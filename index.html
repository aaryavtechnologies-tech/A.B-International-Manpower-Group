ANSWERS TO
YOUR QUESTIONS.

Ask Our Team
Which countries does the agency cover?
Our client-country list features Serbia, Albania, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Russia, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Israel, Maldives, Mauritius, Kuwait, UAE, Oman, Qatar and Jordan. Other previously mentioned destinations include Poland, Romania, Greece and Turkey; confirm the latest availability with our team.
Can I enquire for multiple job types?
Yes. You can mention your relevant skills, prior experience and preferred roles when you contact our team.
Is a job or work visa guaranteed?
No. Job availability, selection and visa outcomes depend on the employer, your eligibility and the relevant government authorities.
What documents should I prepare?
Exact requirements depend on the vacancy and destination. Common items may include a valid passport, CV, experience records and qualifications where applicable. Confirm the full list before submission.
