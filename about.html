A.B International Manpower Group is an employment agency based in Sadulpur, Rajasthan, supporting enquiries for overseas work visa opportunities.

Our communicated services cover multiple regions and work sectors, including hospitality, kitchen operations, technical trades, construction, transport, general support and agriculture. We help candidates understand the kinds of requirements and processes associated with international work.
